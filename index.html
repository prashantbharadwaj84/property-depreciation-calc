Mid-Month Convention for Real Property

Under IRS MACRS guidelines, all real property (residential rental and nonresidential commercial buildings) is subject to the Mid-Month Convention:

  • Prorated Placement: Property is treated as placed in service (or disposed of) in the middle of the month, regardless of the actual calendar date acquired.
  • Year 1 & Final Year Calculation: The first year deduction is calculated as (12 - Month + 0.5) / 12 of the full annual straight-line amount, with the remaining fraction recovered in the final tail year.
Residential (27.5-Yr) vs Commercial (39-Yr)

IRS Publication 527 classifies real estate assets into two primary recovery periods using Straight Line (SL) depreciation:

  • Residential Rental (27.5 Years): Properties where 80% or more of gross rental income comes from dwelling units. Full-year recovery rate is 3.636% per year.
  • Nonresidential Commercial (39 Years): Office, retail, industrial, and warehouse space. Full-year recovery rate is 2.564% per year.
  • Land Exclusion: Land is non-depreciable. Land value must always be subtracted from total purchase price to determine eligible building basis.
